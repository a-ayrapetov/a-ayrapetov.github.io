I am a Cybersecurity Architect based in New York. I design network access control, segmentation and detection for enterprise and industrial networks, and I stay on as technical lead from scoping to handover.

**What I work on**

- Identity and network access control: Cisco ISE 3.x, 802.1X, EAP-TLS, MAB, TACACS+ device administration, PKI and SCEP, TrustSec with SGT, SXP and SGACL.
- OT and ICS security: zones and conduits along IEC 62443 and NIST SP 800-82, passive asset discovery, closed-mode access control on industrial switches, monitoring through TAPs and SPAN for platforms such as Nozomi and Darktrace, hardening of systems that cannot be patched.
- Detection: Cisco Secure Network Analytics, Darktrace, NetFlow and IPFIX design, alarm tuning that keeps a SOC or NOC looking.
- Campus and data center: Catalyst Center and SD-Access, Catalyst 9000 and 9800, Nexus with vPC, VXLAN EVPN.
- Firewalls and WAN: Cisco Secure Firewall, Palo Alto, FortiGate, Meraki MX and vMX, SD-WAN, AWS and Azure connectivity.
- Automation: Python, PowerShell and controller APIs, always read-only first, with the evidence printed next to every result.

**How I work**

Read-only before anything changes. One record per engagement. Every fact traced to output, a vendor document for the exact release, or the record. A change ships with its verification and its rollback. The [playbooks](https://github.com/a-ayrapetov/a-ayrapetov#how-i-work-playbooks) describe this in detail.

**Credentials**

Cisco CCNP Enterprise, with the Enterprise Core and Enterprise Design specialist certifications. CompTIA CySA+, Security+ and Security Analytics Professional (CSAP). Training: Darktrace Academy, Cisco U. AI Technical Practitioner, AI Business Practitioner and Network Automation Essentials.
